# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, responsibility, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, insults, trolling, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to project maintainers through repository security or moderation contacts. Maintainers will investigate fairly, respect reporter privacy, and may correct, warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban contributors according to impact and pattern.

This Code of Conduct adapts Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
